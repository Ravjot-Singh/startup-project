Reminder rules

A reminder goes out when the number of days since the due date equals the rule's offset (negative = before due). A customer gets one message per day, and its tone follows their oldest unpaid bill (e.g. a customer with a 31-day-old bill gets the 30-day notice, even if a newer bill is the one due today).
