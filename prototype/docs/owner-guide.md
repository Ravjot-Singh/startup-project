# How to use the app

The app chases payments on WhatsApp so you don't have to phone people. It sends reminders, bills, thank-you
messages and catalogues automatically. You can also do everything by hand. Most days it takes about
five minutes of your time.

## Your daily five minutes

1. **Upload today's outstanding from Busy.** On Settings, under *Your data*, press **Upload**. In Busy, export the
   bill-wise outstanding (receivables) report to Excel and upload it under *Outstanding bills (upload
   daily)*. If it is the full list, tick *This is the full list*: bills that aren't in it any more are marked
   paid.
2. **Open Today.** The top shows money collected this week, what is due now and what is overdue.
   **Needs you** lists the only things that wait for you:
   - replies from customers;
   - messages that failed;
   - payment promises that were missed;
   - customers 15+ days overdue, where a phone call may help (the **Call list**);
   - customers with dues but no WhatsApp number (**Add numbers**).
3. **Record payments** that came in and aren't in Busy yet (see below).

That's it. Everything under **Going out today** is sent by the app on its own.

## The first time

- **Customers' mobile numbers.** Busy's outstanding report usually has no phone numbers. Once, upload
  the customer (party) list with mobile numbers under *Customer list with mobile numbers* on the same upload page.
- **Check the reminder schedule** under Settings → Reminder schedule. By default there is a gentle message
  before the due date and firmer ones after it. You can change the days, switch steps off or add your own.
- **Set the send time.** Settings → Automatic sending → *Change the send time and sending hours*. Reminders
  start at that hour, and nothing is ever sent outside the sending hours.
- **Bill PDFs (optional).** If you also export bills as PDFs from Busy, upload them under *Bill PDFs from Busy*.
  They are matched by the bill number in the file name. Without them, the app makes a neat one-page bill
  with a UPI QR code.

## Recording a payment

Open the customer (Customers tab, search by name, shop or number), then **Record a payment**:

1. Enter the amount, how it was paid and the date.
2. Under **Which bills does it pay?**, tick the bills and adjust the split if needed.
3. **Save payment.**

Money left over is kept as credit and can be used on later bills (*Put credit against these bills*). Made a
mistake? Under *Payments received*, use **Remove this payment**.

When a later Busy upload shows *less* paid than you recorded, your payment is kept. The bill shows **Busy not
updated yet** so you know to enter it in Busy. Uploads never lower a payment you recorded.

## Reminders

Reminders go out automatically. Each customer gets **one message** that covers all their unpaid bills: what
is due now and the total balance, always after part payments. Nobody gets more than one automatic reminder
a day.

On a customer's page you can also:

- **Send reminder now**: goes straight away.
- **Remind on a date and time**: for example "call back on the 5th". Tick *They promised to pay by this date*
  if they promised: if nothing comes in by then, it shows under Needs you.
- **Pause reminders** for just this customer (for example during a talk about the price).
- **Mark as disputed** on one bill: it is left out of reminders until you clear the dispute.

**Pause all sending** on Settings stops everything automatic at once. Use it if anything ever looks wrong.

## Bills on WhatsApp

After an upload from Busy, each new bill is sent to the customer about 30 minutes later, with the PDF
attached. To send one by hand, use **Send bill** next to the bill on the customer's page. You can switch
the automatic part off under Settings → Bills on WhatsApp.

## When someone pays everything

They get a thank-you straight away. Later, around the time they usually order again, they get an
invitation to order with your current catalogue. If they order before that, the invitation is cancelled.
Change this under Settings → After full payment.

The invitation is an offer, so it only goes to customers who **agreed to offers and catalogues**. Tick that
on the customer's page under *Contact and reminder settings*, and write how they agreed (for example "said
yes on the phone, 12 Nov").

## Promotions

The **Promotions** tab:

- **Products**: add each product with a photo, pack size and price. **Build from my products** makes a
  catalogue PDF (**Build from my … products** under Catalogues). You can also upload a catalogue PDF you already have. The one marked *Current* goes with
  reorder invitations.
- **Leads**: people who aren't customers yet. Add them one by one or import a list. Always note how they
  agreed to hear from you. A lead becomes a customer automatically when a Busy upload has a bill for them.
- **New promotion**:
  1. Choose what to send: a catalogue, or a photo with an offer line.
  2. Choose who gets it: customers, leads or both, by area, salesman or tag.
  3. **Preview** shows how many people get it, who is left out and why, and roughly what WhatsApp will
     charge.
  4. Send now, or schedule it for later.

Messages go out in small batches every few minutes so your number stays in good standing. The promotion's
page shows how many were delivered, read, failed or replied.

Only send offers to people who agreed to them. WhatsApp blocks numbers that customers report as spam.

## The Messages tab

Every message the app has sent or will send is listed here.

| It says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waiting | Will go out at the next chance (within sending hours) |
| Sent / Delivered / Read | On its way / on their phone / they opened it |
| Test mode | Written but not really sent (see below) |
| Failed | WhatsApp refused it, the reason is shown. The app tries 3 times before giving up |
| Not sent | Skipped on purpose: paused, opted out, no number, or already paid today |
| Not needed | Cancelled because nothing was owed any more |

**Replies** from customers appear on Today under Needs you. Press **Done** once you have dealt with one.
If a customer writes **STOP**, the app stops all messages to them (**START** turns them back on).
"Stop promotions" stops only offers and catalogues.

## Test mode and the test group

Until WhatsApp is connected, the app runs in **test mode**: every message is written and listed, but nothing
is sent. Use the first week this way and check the Messages page: right people, right amounts, right days?

When WhatsApp is connected, go to Settings → **Who gets real messages**. Choose *Only these numbers* and add
your own number and a few friendly customers. After a few good days, switch to *Everyone*.

## When something looks wrong

- **A customer didn't get a reminder.** Open their page. The badge at the top shows if they opted out or are
  paused, or have no number. Their messages list shows "Not sent" with the reason.
- **"Failed: not on WhatsApp".** That number has no WhatsApp. Correct the number on the customer's page,
  or phone them.
- **Amounts look wrong.** Check the last upload (Settings → Your data), and the payments on the customer's
  page.
- **Anything else.** Press **Pause all sending** first, then look. Nothing goes out while it is paused.
