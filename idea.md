# Idea

A businessman (a distributor) spends a lot of time calling customers about payments and new orders.
He wants one simple dashboard that does this work for him over WhatsApp.

## What he needs

- **Promote his business:** send his product catalogue and offers to customers and to new people who may become customers.
- **Send bills:** send each bill as a PDF on WhatsApp.
- **Payment reminders:** remind customers automatically, on days he chooses, about the full amount or whatever is still unpaid. He can also set a reminder for a specific customer on a specific day and time.
- **Keep amounts right:** when money comes in, the amount owed goes down, and later reminders ask only for what is left.
- **Ask for the next order:** once a customer has paid everything, thank them and, after a while, invite them to order again.
- **Automatic or by hand:** everything happens on its own, but he can send anything himself at any time, or pause it all.

## How it should feel

Simple and calm. The most important things come first: money collected, money due, who needs his attention today,
and what is going out today. It should work well on his phone.
